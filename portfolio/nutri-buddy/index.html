Nutri Buddy

A food and wellbeing diary for a nutritionist and her clients

Clients keep their diary in a browser or PWA, while the nutritionist sees one structured history, settings, and trends.

Context

Food, wellbeing, and habit data often arrives in fragments through messengers, leaving a specialist to manually assemble the history and assess trends.

What I did

I independently designed and built the product: two-role workflows, UX, the React/PWA interface, data model, authentication and access control, diary, analytics, and deployment.

Outcome

The product is live and currently piloting with a nutritionist and her clients.
