Scribeit

An online lecture becomes a learning resource

As co-founder, I led discovery, positioning, MVP, growth, and content-production automation for an international EdTech product.

Context

Learners needed a simple way to work with long lectures without replaying them or making notes manually.

What I did

I ran 70+ interviews across three segments, built an international MVP, and completed three positioning iterations. I designed an automated flow: lecture URL -> structured notes -> published catalogue page.

Outcome

50+ early users; average search position 25 -> 5; CTR 1.3% -> 3.1%; a positive Business Finland opinion on the business plan and 2 partnership agreements.
